Cyber Safeguarding

Through the internet, you can find lots of useful information and learn new things. It is also a way to talk to friends and show your talents – but you need to watch out! Cyber safeguarding means taking steps to keep yourself and your personal information safe and to avoid online scams.

Benefits vs. Dangers of the Internet

Benefits the internet can bring to you

!

But, as mentioned, it is important to look out, know the risks and stay safe online. We know the internet can be a tricky place, so we want to make sure you know about the dangers and how to protect yourself. Let’s make the internet a safe place for everyone!

Types of Cyber Criminality & Tips and Tricks to Stay Safe
