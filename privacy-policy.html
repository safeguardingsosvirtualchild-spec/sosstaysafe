Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, shared and protected when you visit the SOS StaySafe website.

1About Us

This website is operated by SOS Children’s Villages. The purpose of this website is to provide information, resources and awareness materials related to safeguarding and child protection.

Website address: https://sos-staysafe.com

2Personal Data We Collect

We only collect personal data that is necessary for the functioning of the website and for responding to user requests.

This website has no contact forms and does not ask you to enter any personal information. The personal data involved may include:

  • Information you choose to send us, for example if you email us (such as your name, email address and the content of your message).
  • Technical information, such as your IP address, that is recorded automatically by our hosting provider when you visit the website, for security purposes (see “Data Sharing”).

We do not allow users to create accounts, publish content, or upload media on this website.

3Use of Personal Data

Personal data collected through this website is used only for:

  • Responding to enquiries or requests submitted by users.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Improving website performance and user experience.

Personal data will not be used for marketing purposes unless explicitly stated and consented to.

4Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising tools.

To remember the language you choose, the website saves a small setting in your browser’s local storage. It contains only the language code (for example, “fr” for French), stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the stored data for this website in your browser.

To let pages you have opened work without internet, the website keeps a copy of its pages, design files and small images in your browser’s storage for this website. This copy contains no information about you and is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing the stored data for this website in your browser.

Embedded content from other websites may set its own cookies (see “Embedded Content”).

If analytics or other non-essential cookies are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated and, where required by law, users will be informed and asked for consent.

5Embedded Content

This website includes embedded content from other websites. Embedded content from other websites behaves in the same way as if the visitor had visited those websites directly. These third-party websites may collect data, use cookies or track interactions according to their own privacy policies.

Interactive learning modules

Interactive learning modules made with Genially are embedded on these pages. They load from Genially’s servers when you view that part of the page:

  • Your Rights
  • Preventing Abuse
  • Reporting Abuse
  • Cyber Safeguarding

Genially privacy policy (opens in a new tab)↗

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other websites, such as the SOS SpeakUp reporting channel, the SOS Children’s Villages Ombuds Office and a report by the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC). These websites are not part of SOS StaySafe. They only receive information from you if you open the link, and their own privacy policies apply.

If you report a concern through SpeakUp, the information you provide is handled through that reporting channel, not through this website.

Videos and images

The videos and images on this website are stored with the website itself. They are not loaded from video platforms or other websites.

6Data Sharing

Personal data is not sold or shared with third parties except where necessary for website hosting, security, or when required by law.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes.

The website’s domain name, sos-staysafe.com, is registered and managed through GoDaddy.

GitHub General Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab)↗

7Data Retention

This website does not have contact forms, so it does not store information that you submit.

The “Ask SOS StaySafe” assistant answers only with text already published on this website. What you type is not stored or sent anywhere, and it disappears when you close the assistant.

Personal data that you send us by email is retained only for as long as necessary to respond to the request and for administrative or legal purposes.

Technical records kept by our hosting provider are retained according to that provider’s own policies.

8Your Rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws, users may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. Requests can be made through the contact details provided below.
